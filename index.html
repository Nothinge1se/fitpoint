Who We Are

MORE THAN A GYM. A COMMUNITY.

FitPoint was founded with one belief: every person in Dakshin Dinajpur deserves access to world-class fitness. We brought that belief to life right here in Harirampur.

From our 8,000 sq ft air-conditioned facility to internationally certified coaches, FitPoint offers everything you need — whether you're a first-timer wanting to lose weight, an athlete training for competition, or a senior looking to stay active and healthy.

We're proud to be the region's first gym with a dedicated women's-only zone, a certified nutrition counselor on staff, and 24/7 CCTV-monitored premises for your complete safety.

What We Offer

WORLD-CLASS FACILITIES

Everything you need under one roof — engineered for performance, designed for results.

Strength Training Floor

Over 60 free-weight stations, power racks, Smith machines, and cable systems. Our strength floor rivals facilities in major metros.

Cardio Zone

30+ treadmills, ellipticals, rowing machines, assault bikes, and steppers — all with personal TV screens and USB charging.

Group Class Studio

Sprung wooden floor, mirrored walls, professional sound system. Hosts yoga, Zumba, HIIT, Pilates, and boxing classes daily.

Functional Training Area

Turf track, suspension trainers, kettlebells, battle ropes, plyometric boxes, and sleds for next-level athletic conditioning.

Yoga & Meditation Room

Serene, soundproofed space for yoga, stretching, and mindfulness. Aromatherapy diffusers and natural lighting for total calm.

Women's Exclusive Zone

A fully private, fully equipped workout area for women — with dedicated trainer support in a comfortable, judgment-free space.
